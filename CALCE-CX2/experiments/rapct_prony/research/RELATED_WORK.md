## Actual reference-code inspection

In `ml_solver.py`, `class NPSSL` registers fixed `b_grid`, parameterizes amplitudes with softplus and computes decay exp(-b*dt). Its zero-initialized states accumulate velocity-driven input at each step; outputs sum the modes. The oscillator residual uses displacement derivatives, stiffness and forcing. None supplies a battery degradation law. This project's intended memory adaptation replaces velocity forcing with causal stress descriptors, accepts carried states, separates unknown gaps and bounds capacity penalty. Code has not been copied. Reference main branch is mutable; a pinned source revision/hash remains necessary before publication.

## Equation classification

- Within-step integral of measured current: measurement-based capacity estimate, subject to sampling/counter validation.
- D_next = D + softplus(rate) * cycle_increment: empirical irreversible-aging assumption, not an electrochemical PDE.
- Rate smoothness: mathematical regularizer; not an independently measured law.
- z_after = z_before * exp(-rest/tau): assumed effective linear relaxation, testable synthetically; capacity interpretation requires experimental identification.
- S_ref - D - P: learnable latent decomposition. Offsets, stress amplitudes and multiple timescales may be nonidentifiable from capacity alone.
- Arrhenius, SEI mechanisms and impedance growth: require additional trustworthy measurements/parameters; omitted.

CNN–Transformer fusion, physics-guided SOH, exponential relaxation and degradation decomposition are not individual novelty claims. A possible research gap is jointly testing these components with verified-rest provenance, explicit prediction-time availability and matched nested cross-battery ablations. This is a testable hypothesis, not established publishable novelty. Full-text gaps above prevent a definitive novelty assessment.
