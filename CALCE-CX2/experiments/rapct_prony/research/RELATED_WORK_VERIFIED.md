## Actual NPSSL inspection and differences

The layer registers a fixed logarithmic decay-rate grid, learns softplus amplitudes, starts z at zero per call, updates z = exp(-b*dt) z + a*dt*v, and sums modes. Its default second-based timescale range belongs to vibration experiments and must not become battery time constants.

The battery adaptation must accept and return state, preserve it during active operation, distinguish unknown acquisition gaps, use positive bounded timescales and nonnegative bounded capacity penalties, and initialize unknown histories explicitly. Learned timescales are effective model parameters, not unique reactions. Synthetic time ranges must be labeled synthetic. Experimental ranges can only be fitted from verified training-rest durations.

## Equation taxonomy and research gap

- Current integration with counter agreement: measurement-based capacity estimate with sampling/calibration limitations.
- D_next = D + nonnegative_rate * observed_cycle_increment: latent irreversible-aging assumption.
- Smooth rates and agreement between an independent encoder state and accumulated state: mathematical regularizers, not electrochemical laws or independent measured states.
- Exponential rest decay and nonnegative stress accumulation: assumed effective memory dynamics requiring excitation/identifiability checks.
- Learned reference minus irreversible loss minus bounded transient penalty: nonidentifiable decomposition without additional evidence; initial-state sensitivity must be audited.
- Arrhenius, impedance growth, true SOC and a calibrated SEI law: unsupported here and omitted.

No individual novelty is claimed for CNN-Transformer fusion, PINN SOH, exponential memory or degradation decomposition. The testable contribution is a controlled, cutoff-audited, persistent-memory comparison using genuinely verified rest events. Its novelty and its central recovery advantage remain unestablished until related-work gaps and real-data evidence are resolved.
